Privacy information

NiteCore Archives is a private backup utility. Updated 6 October 2026.

Access and purpose

The utility uses Google's drive.file permission to create and manage its own archive files and files explicitly used with it. It reads these files to verify integrity, find relevant evidence, and restore selected archives. It does not request access to the account's unrelated Drive files.

Data handled

Backups may contain diagnostic logs, recordings, screenshots, dates, source filenames, and customer identifiers already present in the operator's source systems. The archive index includes hashes and relevant context for troubleshooting. Connection credentials are kept outside the archives.

Storage and use

Archives are stored in the connected account's Google Drive. Temporary working copies and private indexes are kept on the operator's computers and server. The utility does not publish backups or sell data, serve advertising, or use archive contents to train a general-purpose AI model. The operator may retrieve selected evidence for human or AI-assisted troubleshooting at the operator's direction.

Retention and control

Source copies may be removed after the upload and its index have been verified. Drive archives are retained until the account owner removes them. The owner can revoke the utility's access through their Google Account connections settings; revocation stops future access but does not delete existing archives.

Contact

Contact the operator who installed the utility or the maintainer. Do not post logs, credentials, or personal information in a public issue.
